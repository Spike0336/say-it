# 🏡 Smart London Townhouse – Kids Game

A fun, interactive drag-and-drop house game for small children.

Children can put **furniture, household items, toys, people and pets** into the rooms of a smart London townhouse.  
They can also **talk to the characters** using the microphone – the characters give short, friendly spoken answers.

## How to Play

1. Open the game in a modern browser (**Chrome, Edge or Safari** work best for the microphone).
2. Drag people, furniture and toys from the right-hand panel into the rooms.
3. Tap a person who is already in a room.
4. Hold the big red 🎤 button and speak (e.g. “What’s your favourite colour?”, “Where are you?”, “Can you dance?”, “Tell me a joke!”).
5. Use the buttons:
   - 🎵 **Music** – cheerful background music
   - 💡 **Smart Magic** – rooms light up + confetti
   - 🎉 **Party Mode** – music + dancing + rainbow rooms
   - 🔄 **Reset** – clear the house and start again

## Rooms
Kitchen • Living Room • Dining Room • Bedroom • Bathroom • Playroom • Attic • Garden & Patio

## Characters
Mum, Dad, Emma, Ollie, Granny, Grandpa, Baby Leo, Auntie Jo, Barkley the dog, Whiskers the cat, Fluffy the rabbit, Robo the robot

## Features
- Fully offline once loaded
- Touch-friendly (works on tablets and phones)
- British English voice replies (when available)
- No external accounts or APIs required

## How to Host on GitHub Pages

1. Create a new repository on GitHub (e.g. `smart-london-house`).
2. Upload the files in this folder (`index.html` and `README.md`).
3. Go to **Settings → Pages**.
4. Under **Source**, choose **Deploy from a branch**.
5. Select the `main` branch and `/ (root)` folder, then click **Save**.
6. After a minute or two your game will be live at:  
   `https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/smart-london-house/`

## Local Testing

Just open `index.html` in your browser – no server needed.
